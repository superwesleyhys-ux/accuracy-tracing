# Admission interpretation: dates and exact falsity

This is an independent interpretation of the existing admission rules, not a new registration or a relaxation. It uses only `CONTINUE.md`, `EVALUATION-NOTES.md`, and the three safe intake STATUS files. No new candidate identity, source, gold, model output or private screening note was inspected. No browsing or model calls were performed. All registered gates, labels and earlier decisions remain unchanged.

## What the permitted records show

The written rules already separate formal confirmation, public doubts, private investigation and first public establishment. They do **not** explicitly require proving that no earlier publication exists anywhere. The safe statuses record unverified chronology alongside missing originals and missing claim-to-finding bindings; they cannot show whether reviewers conflated allegations with establishment. The actionable gap is an explicit, consistently applied chronology rubric. Zero admissions alone does not diagnose an impossible standard.

Continue requiring **both** a public formal finding in 2025–2026 and first public establishment of the specific falsehood in 2025–2026 while the user's interpretation remains unresolved. A formal finding alone does not pass the stricter gate.

## Establishment is about content, not just publication type

Classify every relevant earlier item against the exact target:

| Earlier item | Treatment under the existing strict gate |
|---|---|
| Private complaint, investigation or knowledge | Record separately. It does not itself establish earlier public disclosure. |
| Public suspicion, accusation or unsupported allegation | Record its date and scope. Do not automatically treat it as an established falsehood. |
| Public allegation accompanied by decisive, auditable records | Evaluate the records. The word “allegation” does not immunize an already established factual contradiction. |
| Public evidence-backed finding that establishes the same falsehood | An earlier date defeats the first-establishment-in-2025–2026 gate, even if a formal authority acts later. |
| Finding about another claim, period, document or actor | Does not settle this target without an explicit factual connection. |

Keep the availability of underlying clues separate from a public presentation establishing their consequence for the target. The existence of old source material from which a new analysis *could* discover a problem is not automatically an earlier public finding. Conversely, do not disregard a clear earlier public demonstration merely because it lacks the later authority's wording or institutional status. Freeze the establishment threshold before examining model predictions.

## A practical chronology record

An independent source reviewer should preserve:

1. The exact target and original version, including qualifications; the date the claim existed; and separate proof of the supplied bytes' pre-cutoff availability.
2. The dated primary public finding, the passages and underlying records that establish this target, and its public release date. Decision, investigation, online release and issue dates are different fields; do not invent day-level precision.
3. Separate earliest located dates for public allegation, public evidence-backed establishment and formal finding, plus any relevant private-knowledge date.
4. A bounded search log covering the finding's cited chronology, identifiable earlier complaints or proceedings, relevant public indexes/archives, and targeted searches for the exact proposition. Classify material earlier items rather than merely counting hits.
5. An independent conclusion: **strict gate supported**, **earlier public establishment found**, or **material chronology unresolved**, with the documentary basis and remaining uncertainty.

Firstness is an empirical historical conclusion, not a mathematical proof of a universal negative. A dated decisive finding, informative documentary chronology and completed targeted checks can support that conclusion without a claim of exhaustive knowledge of every publication. But a bounded search returning no hits is not sufficient by itself, and “first” must not silently become “first found in our search.” When the primary chronology is missing, an earlier evidence-bearing record is inaccessible, or the date distinction could change eligibility, retain a conditional lead with that specific blocker. Do not require endless searching merely because absolute certainty is impossible.

## Literal falsity is not interchangeable with misleading presentation

Read the complete original advertisement and the actual finding's reasons. Classify the finding as establishing **literal factual falsity**, **a misleading impression or omission**, **lack of substantiation**, or **another violation without a settled truth finding**. These categories cannot automatically share a falsehood label.

A correct stated rate can coexist with a misleading overall price impression caused by an omitted condition. A finding about that impression does not make every printed amount false. Do not strengthen a qualified rate into an all-inclusive total, expand a bounded statement into a universal claim, or infer fabricated transactions from an advertising violation. Bind any claimed false proposition to the actual advertisement's wording or an independently justified conveyed proposition, and to the specific facts established in the decision.

If the evidence establishes only misleading presentation, keep that result outside a literal-falsity benchmark. A separate misleading-impression endpoint would require explicit prospective registration, an exact target and appropriate labels; it cannot repair this gate retroactively. If type size, placement or omitted visual context decides the interpretation, a text extraction alone is insufficient. Preserve the relevant original visual context and supply it comparably, or retain the case as not ready for that task.

## Admission decision and limits

Use separate blockers for unavailable original bytes, uncertain target scope, missing exact falsity, formal-date-only support, earlier decisive public establishment, and materially unresolved chronology. Earlier allegations are chronology context unless their content meets the establishment threshold. Optional domain/year matching must not be reintroduced as a mandatory barrier.

Admit only when the original-version, exact-truth and both date gates independently pass. This note admits no case and changes no label. If later evidence contradicts an admission chronology, preserve the original trial and record a separately reviewed correction; never silently overwrite the benchmark. A truthful assessment of uncertainty is preferable to either indefinite impossible-proof demands or a favorable-date assumption.
